## Full Description

### What is CorridorWork?

CorridorWork is a compliance-first cross-border hiring platform that structures international workforce interest for employers, candidates and partner organisations. It is technically complete, publicly live at corridorwork.com, and ready for a buyer to operate or build upon.

The platform was built with a deliberate "inbound-first, no-cold-outreach, GDPR-compliant" approach:
- Employers submit workforce demand via a structured intake form
- Candidates express interest voluntarily across sectors and corridors
- Partners (agencies, training providers, mobility partners) can collaborate via structured forms
- All submissions are manually reviewed — no automatic matching, no automatic emails

### Who is this for?

- A **recruiting agency** or **HR tech operator** who wants an instant cross-border hiring platform to white-label or operate under their brand
- An **investor or operator** looking for a technical asset in the international mobility / talent corridor space
- A **migration or mobility consultancy** that wants a compliant, inbound-first digital intake system
- A **developer or product team** who wants a fully-built, tested Next.js 14 SaaS starter in a real market niche
