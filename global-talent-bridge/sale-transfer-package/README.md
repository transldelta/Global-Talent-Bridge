## What is CorridorWork?

CorridorWork is a live, fully-built SaaS platform for structured cross-border hiring. It connects employer demand with international candidate interest across sectors, regions and talent corridors — for employers, candidates and partner organisations.

The platform is technically complete, publicly deployed and buyer-ready. It is sold as a **software asset**, not as an operating business with active customers or revenue.

## Compliance Statements

- **No job guarantee** — The platform makes no guarantees of employment for any candidate.
- **No visa guarantee** — No immigration advice or visa services are provided.
- **No placement guarantee** — No automated or guaranteed job placements.
- **No payment processing active** — Stripe and payment flows are not configured.
- **No automated outreach** — All email and outreach systems are set to `none` by default.
- **No automated emails** — `EMAIL_PROVIDER=none`, `OUTREACH_EMAIL_PROVIDER=none`.
- **GDPR-compliant** — EU-region Supabase, consent-based forms, no data sharing.
