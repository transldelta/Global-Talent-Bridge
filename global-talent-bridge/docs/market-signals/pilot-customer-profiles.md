## Profile 1 — German Hospital Group

**Corridor:** Germany Healthcare (Buyer Opportunity Score: 93)

**Who they are:** A regional German hospital group or care facility operator actively recruiting nurses, doctors, or care staff internationally.

**Core problem:** International recruiting is slow, expensive, and filled with compliance uncertainty. HR teams lack structured corridor intelligence to make source-market decisions confidently.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The Germany Healthcare corridor card maps source markets, partner channels, compliance pathways, and candidate supply — with Buyer Opportunity Score rationale.

**Proof question:**
> "Would a structured corridor intelligence briefing — showing the top 3 source markets for your open nursing or care roles, ranked by speed-to-hire and compliance simplicity — be useful to your HR team?"

**Pilot scope (if interest confirmed):**
- 1-hour corridor intelligence briefing (manual, no automated delivery)
- Sharing relevant corridor data from the admin tool
- Documented feedback from the HR team as market signal evidence

**Compliance note:**
- No job guarantee. No visa guarantee. No placement fee arrangement without legal review.
- Any pilot engagement is intelligence and advisory only.
- The organisation must run its own legal compliance for actual hiring.

## Profile 2 — German Engineering / Manufacturing Firm

**Corridor:** Germany Engineering (Buyer Opportunity Score: 87)

**Who they are:** A Mittelstand engineering or manufacturing company struggling to fill skilled trades or engineering roles domestically.

**Core problem:** Domestic talent shortage is structural. International hiring feels legally complex and risky without a structured guide.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** Corridor Intelligence simplifies the decision — which country, which partner, which timeline — without the firm needing a dedicated international HR expert.

**Proof question:**
> "If we could show you the top 3 international source markets for [role type], ranked by compliance simplicity and time-to-onboard — would that help you make the case internally for international hiring?"

**Compliance note:** No placement guarantee. No visa processing. Advisory and intelligence only. Legal counsel required for actual hiring.

## Profile 3 — UK Hospitality Operator (Post-Brexit)

**Corridor:** UK Hospitality (Buyer Opportunity Score: 81)

**Who they are:** A UK hotel group, restaurant chain, or hospitality operator with post-Brexit staffing gaps.

**Core problem:** Right-to-work complexity, visa uncertainty, and lack of structured access to EU or non-EU candidate sources.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The UK Hospitality corridor maps legal pathways, candidate source markets (Southern Europe, Latin America), and partner channels specifically for this sector.

**Proof question:**
> "Are you currently exploring international hiring as a solution to post-Brexit staffing gaps — and if so, what is your biggest obstacle right now?"

**Compliance note:** No visa processing or legal advice. Right-to-work verification is the employer's responsibility. No placement guarantee.

## Profile 4 — Netherlands Logistics / Warehousing

**Corridor:** Netherlands Logistics (Buyer Opportunity Score: 79)

**Who they are:** A Dutch logistics, distribution, or warehousing company hiring warehouse operators, drivers, or supply chain staff internationally.

**Core problem:** Logistics operators need volume hiring — finding reliable partner channels who understand Dutch labour law is the main bottleneck.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The partner intake maps recruiting agencies and relocation firms already operating in this corridor. The Corridor Intelligence tool ranks them by reliability and compliance simplicity.

**Proof question:**
> "What partner channels are you currently using for international logistics hiring — and what's your main frustration with them?"

**Compliance note:** No placement guarantee. Dutch employment law compliance is the operator's responsibility. No visa guidance.

## Profile 5 — Language School / Training Provider

**Corridor:** Multiple (all corridors include language preparation stage)

**Who they are:** A German, Austrian, or Swiss language school or vocational training provider preparing international candidates for employment in DACH.

**Core problem:** Language schools graduate candidates but lack structured access to employer demand. They don't know which sectors are actively hiring their graduates.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The employer intake + corridor intelligence shows language schools exactly which employers, sectors, and corridors their candidates should target — turning a supply-side provider into a demand-aware pipeline.

**Proof question:**
> "Do you currently have a structured channel for connecting your graduates with employers actively hiring internationally — and if not, what would that connection be worth to your school?"

**Compliance note:** No job guarantee for graduates. Candidate intake is voluntary, consent-based, and free. No candidate fees. No visa guidance.

## What counts as a pilot signal

Only record as market proof if:

- ✅ The organisation responds in writing (email, LinkedIn message)
- ✅ They answer the proof question substantively
- ✅ They express interest in a follow-up conversation or briefing
- ✅ You can provide the written evidence to a potential buyer

Does **NOT** count:
- ❌ Verbal conversations without written record
- ❌ "Yes that sounds interesting" without specific engagement
- ❌ Internal test users
- ❌ Your own test accounts
