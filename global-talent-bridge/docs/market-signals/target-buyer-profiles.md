- No fake buyers. No invented contact details. No fabricated interest.

## Profile 1 — HR-Tech SaaS Company

**Who they are:** A B2B SaaS company operating in talent acquisition, workforce management, or HR automation.

**Why relevant:** Has existing GTM motion, customer relationships, and engineering capacity to integrate corridor intelligence into their product or acquire it as a module.

**Core problem:** Lacks structured international corridor data to help their customers expand hiring across borders.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The 8-corridor intelligence engine + three-sided intake pipeline can be integrated into their product or acquired and operated as a standalone SKU.

**Proof question:**
> "Would structured corridor intelligence data — scored corridors, employer demand intake, partner channels — be something your customers would pay for as an add-on?"

**Compliance note:** State pre-revenue status. No MRR claims. No customer claims. Transfer guide available for technical due diligence.

## Profile 2 — International Recruiting Agency

**Who they are:** A recruiting firm specialising in cross-border placements — healthcare workers, engineers, skilled trades, IT.

**Why relevant:** Operates in exactly the corridors CorridorWork covers. Could use the platform as an internal tool or white-label it for clients.

**Core problem:** Lacks structured corridor-level intelligence to advise clients on where international hiring is viable, fast, and legally straightforward.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** Corridor Intelligence + Approval Queue + Contact Copilot maps directly onto their daily workflow without requiring a new tech team.

**Proof question:**
> "Would a corridor intelligence tool — showing employer demand, candidate supply, and compliance complexity per country-pair — improve how you advise your clients?"

**Compliance note:** Recruitment intermediary rules vary by jurisdiction. No placement guarantee. No visa guidance. Buyer must conduct own legal review.

## Profile 3 — Relocation / Talent Mobility Firm

**Who they are:** A firm providing relocation services, visa support, or mobility programmes for employers moving international hires.

**Why relevant:** Operates upstream of placement — needs early-stage corridor intelligence to identify growing markets before their employer clients ask.

**Core problem:** Reactive rather than proactive — relocation firms often discover new corridors after employers have already committed.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** Corridor scoring gives relocation firms advance intelligence on which corridors are building employer demand 6–12 months ahead.

**Proof question:**
> "Would early-stage corridor intelligence — scoring which country-pairs are building hiring momentum — be useful for planning your service capacity?"

**Compliance note:** No visa guarantee. No guarantee that any corridor will produce demand. Framed as intelligence tool only.

## Profile 4 — Micro-SaaS Operator / Acquirer

**Who they are:** An independent operator who buys and runs small SaaS products, typically with 1–3 person teams.

**Why relevant:** Has operating discipline and can activate CorridorWork's monetisation paths faster than a solo founder building from scratch.

**Core problem:** Looking for acquisition targets with solid technical foundations and clear niche positioning.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** CorridorWork is transfer-prepared with documented handover steps; actual transfer timing depends on domain, GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, and buyer-side account setup. Latest lint, TypeScript, build, and test suite passed. Full documentation. Clear niche.

**Proof question:**
> "Are you currently evaluating small pre-revenue SaaS acquisitions in HR-tech, recruiting, or talent mobility?"

**Compliance note:** Pre-revenue status must be stated clearly. No MRR claims. No customer claims. Escrow-first transfer only.

---

## Profile 5 — SaaS Portfolio Investor / Micro-PE

**Who they are:** A small private equity firm or operator-investor acquiring a portfolio of micro-SaaS businesses.

**Why relevant:** Asset-stage acquisitions at Tier 1 pricing (€15K–€25K) fit their acquisition criteria; they assign an operator after purchase.

**Core problem:** Evaluating many assets simultaneously — needs clear transfer readiness, technical quality, and honest gap disclosure to make fast decisions.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** Full Buyer Package, 6-dimension Sale Readiness Score, latest lint/build/test suite passed, and escrow-first transfer reduces due diligence time significantly.

**Proof question:**
> "Do you currently have an operator you'd assign to an international talent tech asset if you acquired it?"

**Compliance note:** No revenue guarantee. No growth projection. Asset-stage pricing must be stated. Escrow-first transfer only.
