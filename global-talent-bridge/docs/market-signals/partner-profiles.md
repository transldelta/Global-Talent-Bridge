## Profile 1 — International Recruitment Agency

**Track:** Agency Partner

**Who they are:** A recruiting firm specialising in cross-border placements that wants access to corridor intelligence to improve their service quality and win more clients.

**Core problem:** Agencies operate corridor-by-corridor but lack a structured framework for comparing corridor attractiveness, compliance complexity, or employer demand concentration. Recommendations are experience-based, not data-driven.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The partner intelligence package gives agencies corridor scores, employer demand intake data, and a structured pipeline tool — without replacing their existing CRM.

**Proof question:**
> "Would corridor-level intelligence — scoring corridors by employer demand, compliance simplicity, and candidate supply — improve how you pitch your service to employer clients?"

**Partnership model options:**
- Intelligence subscriber (monthly data access)
- White-label licence (corridor intelligence under their brand)
- Referral partner (introduce employers in exchange for commission)

**Compliance note:** Agency must conduct own compliance and legal review per jurisdiction. No revenue guarantee. No placement volume guarantee. No exclusivity.

## Profile 2 — Relocation Service Provider

**Track:** Relocation Partner

**Who they are:** A firm providing visa assistance, housing, cultural integration, or logistics support for international hires arriving in a destination country.

**Core problem:** Relocation firms are reactive — they engage once an employer has committed. They want earlier access to pipeline to plan capacity and grow proactively.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** Corridor intelligence provides 6–12 month advance signal on which corridors are building employer demand, allowing relocation firms to prepare service capacity before demand peaks.

**Proof question:**
> "Would access to advance corridor demand signal — showing which country-pairs are likely to generate employer hiring volume in the next 6 months — help you plan your service capacity?"

**Partnership model options:**
- Early intelligence subscriber
- Listed partner in partner intake (receive employer referrals for their corridor)

**Compliance note:** No visa processing or legal advice provided by CorridorWork. Relocation firm's own compliance responsibility. No placement guarantee.

## Profile 3 — Language School / B1–B2 Preparation Provider

**Track:** Candidate Source Partner

**Who they are:** A language school preparing international candidates for German, English, Dutch, or French language certification as a prerequisite for employment in DACH or UK/NL.

**Core problem:** Language schools don't know which sectors or roles their graduates should target. Employer partnerships are informal and unstructured. Graduates complete courses but lack employment pathways.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The employer intake captures active hiring demand. Corridor scores show language schools which sectors are absorbing their candidate profiles — turning demand data into a placement advantage.

**Proof question:**
> "Do you have a structured employer relationship programme for placing your graduates — and if not, would you value a tool that shows you where employer demand is highest for your graduate profile?"

**Partnership model options:**
- Candidate source partner (list your school in the candidate source intake)
- Intelligence subscriber (employer demand data for their graduating cohorts)

**Compliance note:** No job guarantee for graduates. Candidate intake is voluntary, consent-based, and free. No candidate fees. No visa guidance.

## Profile 4 — HR Consulting Firm

**Track:** Strategic / Consulting Partner

**Who they are:** An HR consultancy or workforce planning firm advising mid-to-large employers on international talent strategy.

**Core problem:** HR consultants advise on international hiring but lack structured data to benchmark corridors objectively. Recommendations are experience-based, which is harder to defend to boards and procurement teams.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The Buyer Opportunity Score framework gives HR consultants an evidence-backed structure for corridor recommendations — and a demonstrable tool to show clients.

**Proof question:**
> "When advising a client on international hiring strategy, what data would make your corridor recommendation more compelling and defensible to the board?"

**Partnership model options:**
- Intelligence tool licence (use corridor scores in their consulting work)
- White-label (deliver corridor intelligence under their consultancy brand)

**Compliance note:** No advice on employment law. No visa or immigration guidance. Consultant's own professional liability applies to their advice.

---

## Profile 5 — Strategic Tech / ATS Integration Partner

**Track:** Technology Partner

**Who they are:** An Applicant Tracking System (ATS), HRIS, or workforce management platform that wants to integrate corridor intelligence as a feature.

**Core problem:** ATS and HRIS platforms have employer relationships and workflows but lack structured international corridor intelligence. Their customers ask for international hiring support but the platforms have no data layer for it.

**Why CorridorWork fits:** The TypeScript-strict API layer and Supabase data model are designed for clean integration. The corridor intelligence engine can be embedded or white-labelled into their existing product.

**Proof question:**
> "Is international hiring intelligence a feature request you hear from your customers — and would a corridor intelligence API be something you'd consider integrating?"

**Partnership model options:**
- API integration licence
- White-label OEM agreement
- Acquisition (full platform acquisition by the tech partner)

**Compliance note:** No automated data sharing without explicit integration agreement. No auto-send from any integration. GDPR data processing agreement required before any data exchange.

## What counts as a partner signal

Only record as market proof if:

- ✅ The partner responds in writing to the proof question
- ✅ They express concrete interest in one of the partnership model options
- ✅ Written evidence exists that can be shared with a platform buyer

Does **NOT** count:
- ❌ Informal verbal conversations
- ❌ "Sounds interesting" without follow-up commitment
- ❌ LinkedIn likes or reactions
- ❌ Internal referrals from your own network without written record
