*Sprint: Sell-Ready Buyer Room · 2026-06-08*  
*Duration: ~10 minutes · 10 steps*

---

## Before You Start

- Open corridorwork.com in a clean browser tab (no admin session)
- Have `/admin/corridor-intelligence` ready in a second tab (logged in)
- Have `/admin/global-growth-department` ready in a third tab
- Have `/admin/buyer-room` ready in a fourth tab

**Tone:** Confident but honest. Show the product clearly. Do not oversell. Acknowledge the gaps proactively — it builds trust.

## Handling Objections

**"There are no customers."**  
> "Correct. This is an asset-stage acquisition. You're buying the infrastructure, the intelligence framework, and the transfer-ready platform — not a revenue stream. The suggested ask reflects that honestly."

**"Why are you selling?"**  
> "The platform is at the point where scaling requires dedicated commercial focus. A buyer with existing agency or HR tech relationships can activate the monetization paths much faster than solo development."

**"What's the technical quality like?"**  
> "4 112 tests, all green. TypeScript strict. ESLint clean. Build clean. You can run `npm test` yourself in the first 10 minutes of due diligence."

**"What about legal compliance?"**  
> "The platform is designed to be compliant by default. No auto-send, no scraping, no guarantees. Legal review of recruitment intermediary rules in your specific jurisdictions is recommended before paid placement — that's the buyer's responsibility, as it would be for any recruitment business."

**"Can I negotiate the price?"**  
> "Yes. The suggested ask is 49 000 – 79 000 €. Where we land depends on whether you can demonstrate a clear activation path and what market proof exists at time of agreement."
