## Seven monetization paths

None are active yet. The buyer activates them.

| Path | Type | Effort | Activation requirement |

| Employer pilot package | One-time fee | Low | 1 employer conversation |
| Partner intelligence package | Recurring | Low | 1 agency or relocation firm conversation |

---

## Path 1 — Employer pilot package (fastest to activate)

**Revenue type:** One-time fee (€1 000–€5 000 per employer)  
**Effort:** Low — no infrastructure changes needed  
**What it is:** A paid 3–6 month onboarding engagement. The employer gets structured corridor intelligence, a curated candidate pipeline, and access to the admin tools for their specific hiring corridor.

**Activation steps:**
1. Identify 3–5 target employers (already structured in Growth Department)
2. Send outreach via Contact Copilot (manual, no auto-send)
3. Offer a paid pilot package — one-time fee, defined scope
4. Run the pilot using existing admin tools

---

## Path 2 — Partner intelligence package

**Revenue type:** Recurring (€300–€1 500/month per partner)  
**Effort:** Low — intelligence is already built  
**What it is:** Recruiting agencies and relocation firms pay for access to corridor intelligence data — which corridors are active, which employer sectors are hiring, which candidate pools are ready.

**Activation steps:**
1. Identify 3–5 recruiting agencies or relocation firms
2. Offer a corridor intelligence subscription
3. Deliver via read-only Buyer Room access or PDF exports

---

## Path 3 — SaaS licence for recruiting agencies

**Revenue type:** Monthly/annual recurring (€200–€800/month per agency)  
**Effort:** Low–Medium  
**What it is:** Agencies get full platform access — corridor intelligence + growth department + contact copilot. They use it to run their own talent corridor operations.

**Activation steps:**
1. Sign 1–3 agency pilot customers
2. Create agency-specific admin accounts
3. Run onboarding session (1 hour)

---

## Path 4 — White-label licence

**Revenue type:** Annual licence (€5 000–€20 000/year)  
**Effort:** Medium  
**What it is:** A large recruiting firm or HR-tech company licenses the platform under their own brand. Requires UI customisation (logo, colours, domain).

**Activation steps:**
1. Identify 1–2 large partners
2. Negotiate licence terms
3. Apply white-label customisation (Tailwind theme + logo)

---

## Path 5 — Candidate source partnership

**Revenue type:** Recurring (€500–€2 000/month per partner)  
**Effort:** Medium  
**What it is:** Language schools, universities, and training providers pay for access to employer demand data from the active corridors — helping them align their programs with market needs.

---

## Path 6 — Corridor intelligence dashboard (enterprise)

**Revenue type:** Annual licence (€10 000–€50 000/year)  
**Effort:** High  
**What it is:** Enterprise HR-tech companies, government workforce agencies, or investment firms license the full corridor intelligence framework as a B2B intelligence product.
