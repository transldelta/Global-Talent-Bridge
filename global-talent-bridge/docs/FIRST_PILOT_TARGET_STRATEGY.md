# CorridorWork — First Pilot Target Strategy

**Version:** 1.0 · June 2026
**Status:** Active · Phase 1 Pilot
**Admin tool:** https://corridorwork.com/admin/pilot-targets

---

## Goal

Identify, qualify, and prepare manual outreach for the first 1–3 pilot contacts.
No automation. No guarantees. No payment required.

The first pilot is about validation, not revenue.

---

## Which Target Groups to Approach First

### Priority 1 — Employers with genuine workforce demand

**Why first:** Employers with unfilled roles are the most direct product fit.
They have a real problem, a budget context, and motivation to try something new.

**Best sectors for first pilot:**
1. **Healthcare & Nursing** — highest documented international shortage in DACH region
2. **IT & Software** — strong international hiring culture, decision-makers accessible via LinkedIn
3. **Engineering & Manufacturing** — concrete role requirements, measurable criteria
4. **Skilled Trades** — persistent shortages, often frustrated with existing solutions
5. **Construction** — seasonal and structural demand, international openness growing
6. **Hospitality** — language skills critical, international candidates common
7. **Logistics & Transport** — scale-appropriate pilot roles

**How to prioritise:**
- Start with one sector you have a real contact in
- Prefer companies where you know someone personally or have a warm connection
- Avoid companies where you have no way to verify their identity

---

### Priority 2 — Recruiting & Staffing Partners

**Why second:** A single agency partner can open access to 10+ employer clients.
They already understand international placements and the value of structured matching.

**Best partner types:**
- Recruiting agencies with Germany/Austria/Switzerland focus
- Language schools that place graduates with employers
- Vocational training providers with international cohorts
- Relocation firms working with HR teams
- Staffing agencies with cross-border track record

**How to prioritise:**
- Look for agencies that already advertise international placement services
- Approach via their partnership or business development contact
- Use the `/partners` page as the landing point

---

### Priority 3 — Strategic Partners / White-Label Candidates

**Why third:** Highest potential value, but longer sales cycle.
Best approached only after you have at least one employer or agency pilot underway.

**Best strategic partner types:**
- HR tech companies looking for international corridor infrastructure
- Staffing groups wanting a white-label matching tool
- Private equity or family offices with HR tech portfolio interest
- Government-adjacent bodies funding labour market programmes
- Large employer groups wanting an internal tool

**How to approach:**
- Formal email with the due diligence package
- Reference the live deployment, test results, and technical architecture
- Use `/strategic-partnership` as the landing point

## How to Manually Prioritise

Use this scoring approach for each potential target:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Real workforce demand | 20 pts |
| Clear sector | 15 pts |
| International hiring openness | 20 pts |
| Professional website or verifiable presence | 15 pts |
| Contact person available | 15 pts |
| No risky promises requested | 10 pts |
| Clear pilot value | 5 pts |

**Score 70–100:** Strong fit — prepare outreach
**Score 40–69:** Possible fit — gather more information first
**Score 0–39:** Poor fit — skip or deprioritise

---

## Which Messages Are Appropriate

| Channel | When to use |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn message | Warm or semi-warm professional contact; under 300 characters; direct |
| Email | Cold or formal contact; HR team inbox; formal sector (healthcare, legal, finance) |
| WhatsApp | Only genuine personal contacts; never cold; short and specific |
| Strategic partner email | Agencies, HR tech, PE interest; formal; include platform details |

**All messages must be:**
- Signed: `CorridorWork Team`
- Free of job or visa guarantees
- Clear that the pilot is free and non-binding
- Sent manually — never automated

Use the `/admin/pilot-targets` tool to generate and copy the appropriate draft.

---

## Which Risks to Avoid

| Risk | What to do |
|---|---|
| Target wants job guarantee | Decline politely — use legal risk response draft |
| Target wants visa guarantee | Decline — outside our scope |
| Target wants candidate fees | Hard stop — not permitted |
| Unverifiable company | Do not proceed until identity confirmed |
| Mass placement expectation | Clarify our model is not bulk delivery |
| Legal ambiguity (AÜG) | Pause and seek legal advice before proceeding |
| Automated outreach pressure | Decline — our model is manual by design |

## Fastest Safe Path to First Pilot Discussion

```
1. Pick one sector where you have a personal or warm contact
      ↓
2. Open /admin/pilot-targets → fill in Target Card
      ↓
3. Run Fit Score → must be ≥ 70 before proceeding
      ↓
4. Check Red Flags → stop if any high-severity flag is present
      ↓
5. Select appropriate outreach draft → copy manually
      ↓
6. Send manually via LinkedIn, email, or WhatsApp
      ↓
7. If they respond positively → guide to /admin/first-pilot for next steps
      ↓
8. If they register → use /admin/first-pilot Step 4 checklist
      ↓
9. Schedule a 30-minute feedback call
      ↓
10. Document what works and what does not
```

**One qualified, reviewed, manually-contacted target is worth more than
100 untested scraped leads.**
