## 3. CANVA VIDEO (Template + AI)

> Canva: https://canva.com
> Recommended: Use "Video" templates, then customize with CorridorWork brand colors

---

### Setup Instructions

1. Open Canva → New Design → Video (1920×1080 for 16:9)
2. Search templates: "Corporate global intro" or "Tech company promo"
3. Apply CorridorWork brand colors:
   - Primary: `#0F172A` (deep navy)
   - Accent: `#6366F1` (electric indigo)
   - Text: `#FFFFFF` (white) / `#94A3B8` (slate)
4. Replace template text with script from `CORRIDORWORK_PROMO_MASTER_EN.md`

---

**Scene — Professional Team:**

### Canva Text Animations (Use for Screen Text)

For each scene's screen text, use:
- **Animation:** "Fade" or "Rise" (clean, professional)
- **Duration:** 2–3 seconds per text block
- **Font:** Canva's "Raleway Bold" or "Montserrat Bold"
- **Color:** White on dark, or Indigo on white
