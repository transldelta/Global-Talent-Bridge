## Why Market Proof Matters

For a buyer, market proof is the single most valuable signal that the platform concept is validated. Even **1–3 real written replies** from employers, agencies, or language schools transform the asset from "promising codebase" to "validated market approach."

Market proof raises the asking price. It shortens due diligence. It reduces buyer risk.

## The Plan (6 Steps)

### Step 1 — Prepare target batch (Week 1)

Use the **Global Growth Department** (`/admin/global-growth-department`):

1. Open **Batch Target Planner**
2. Select corridor: `Morocco / Tunisia → DACH / France / Spain` (highest Buyer Opportunity Score)
3. Select target type: **Language schools** (Morocco/Tunisia) — fastest activation point
4. Generate batch of 10–20 targets
5. Add additional 10–20 **German healthcare employer** targets

Total: 20–50 targets prepared, reviewed, and staged in approval queue.

### Step 2 — Review messages manually

For each target:
1. Open message in Contact Copilot (`/admin/contact-copilot`)
2. Review generated draft — edit as needed
3. Approve in queue (status: `approved`)

**No messages are sent automatically. Manual review required for every message.**

### Step 3 — Send manually (Gmail Draft / Contact Form)

For each approved message:
- **Email:** use Gmail Draft link (opens pre-filled draft in Gmail — manual send only)
- **Form:** use Form Autofill Helper (fills contact form fields — manual submit only)

Target: 20–50 messages sent over 5–10 working days.

### Step 4 — Collect replies

Track replies in the growth pipeline:
- Update target status to `interested` when reply received
- Save reply content (anonymised if needed) in notes field

**Target: 1–3 written replies within 4 weeks of outreach start.**

### Step 5 — Document as market proof

For buyer conversations:
- Screenshot or quote (anonymised) any written replies
- Note reply context: which corridor, which sector, which message type
- Do not fabricate or embellish any responses

### Step 6 — Use feedback for valuation

Each real reply:
- Validates the corridor approach
- Raises the sale readiness score (market proof criterion)
- Justifies higher end of suggested ask range (49 000 – 79 000 €)
- Can be shared with buyer in due diligence (with contact's permission)
