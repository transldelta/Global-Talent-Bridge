# Buyer Question & Answer Bank — CorridorWork

*Sale Process · Buyer Response Kit · Admin use only*

---

## How to use this

These are honest, pre-drafted answers to common buyer questions. Read the relevant
answer, personalise if needed, and send manually. Do not copy-paste without reviewing.

No answers promise revenue, customers, placements, jobs, or visas.

---

## Q1: Why are you selling?

**Honest answer:**

> I am a solo founder / owner who built CorridorWork as a structured infrastructure
> product for the international talent mobility market. I am prioritising other
> projects and do not have the market relationships or GTM capacity to activate
> CorridorWork commercially at this stage.
>
> A buyer with existing distribution in HR-tech, international recruiting, or
> talent mobility can activate this infrastructure faster than I can. That is the
> honest reason for the sale.

**What not to say:**
- Do not say "the business is struggling" — it was never commercially activated.
- Do not say "I have other investors pressuring me" — untrue.
- Do not fabricate urgency.

---

## Q2: Is there revenue?

**Honest answer:**

> No. CorridorWork is pre-revenue. €0 MRR, €0 ARR. No paying customers, no paid
> subscriptions, no commercial transactions of any kind.
>
> The sale is asset-based, not revenue-multiple based. The asking price reflects
> the built infrastructure — not a revenue stream.

**What not to say:**
- Do not round up or imply "minimal revenue" — revenue is exactly €0.
- Do not describe test data or internal usage as revenue.

---

## Q3: Are there customers?

**Honest answer:**

> No. There are 0 paying customers and 0 signed agreements with any organisation.
> The platform has functioning intake forms for employers, candidates, and partners,
> but no one has gone through a full commercial onboarding process.
>
> There are no letters of intent, no pilot agreements, and no committed buyers.

**What not to say:**
- Do not describe people who visited the site as "customers."
- Do not describe test submissions as "leads" unless they are real, named organisations
  that expressed specific, documented interest.
