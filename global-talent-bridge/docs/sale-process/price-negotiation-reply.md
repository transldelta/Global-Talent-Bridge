# Price Negotiation Reply — CorridorWork

*Sale Process · Buyer Response Kit · Admin use only*

---

## When to use this

Use this template when a buyer asks about pricing, makes a counter-offer, or challenges
the asking price.

**Core position:** The asking price is asset-based, not revenue-multiple based.
CorridorWork has €0 MRR and 0 customers. The price reflects the built and
documented infrastructure. Be honest. Do not inflate claims to justify a higher number.

---

## Template — Responding to a price question

---

**Subject:** Re: CorridorWork — pricing

Hi [Name],

Happy to discuss pricing directly.

The asking price for CorridorWork is positioned as follows:

**Current realistic range: €15 000 – €25 000**

This reflects an asset-based valuation — not a revenue multiple. The reasoning:

- CorridorWork is pre-revenue: €0 MRR, 0 paying customers
- The value is the built infrastructure: codebase, domain, deployment, documentation,
  and corridor intelligence engine
- A buyer with existing market relationships in HR-tech, international recruiting, or
  talent mobility can activate this infrastructure without building it from scratch
- No revenue guarantee, no customer guarantee, no placement guarantee

The price is negotiable. I am open to serious written offers.

**What affects the final price:**
- Buyer's ability to close quickly and manage the transfer process independently
- Whether the buyer brings documented market interest (LOIs, pilot customers) that
  validates the corridor intelligence product
- Transfer terms agreed: asset-only vs. any limited advisory period

**What does not affect the price:**
- Future revenue projections — I will not make them
- Promises about growth trajectory — I will not make them
- Job, visa, or placement guarantees — not applicable

If you would like to make an offer, please do so in writing with your proposed
price and any conditions. I will respond in writing.

Kind regards,
[Your name]
CorridorWork

## Notes for the founder

- Do not accept payment outside escrow for any amount. Even for small amounts.
  See `red-lines.md`.
- Do not negotiate verbally without confirming the agreement in writing afterwards.
- If a buyer asks for a discount in exchange for a fast close: a fast close is
  actually worth something to you — factor that in honestly.
- The 3-tier pricing ladder (Tier 1: €15K–€25K, Tier 2: €30K–€50K, Tier 3: €50K–€69K+)
  is documented in `docs/buyer-package/asking-price-rationale.md`. Higher tiers
  require documented buyer signals or real MRR — do not claim them without evidence.
- If a buyer walks away: that is acceptable. Do not lower your red-line price below
  what you are genuinely comfortable with.
